{% endblock %} {% block page_instructions %}

Create accounts individually or import them from CSV. Assign the least-privileged role that supports their work. For an imported account without a temporary password, select Issue code and share the code securely with the account holder.

  • Passwords must be at least 15 characters and include uppercase and lowercase letters, a number, and a special character.
  • Administrators and treasurers must enroll an authenticator app at their first login.
  • Password and MFA resets require offline identity verification and sign out all target sessions.
  • Volunteers must be linked to a person; treasurers may be linked to a person to enter trips for themselves.
  • Admins can enter trips for any person. Other roles can only enter trips for their linked person.
