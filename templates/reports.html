This report summarizes recorded reimbursement payments, active donations, estimated Gift Aid, and trip mileage by volunteer.

  • Expenses paid are the total of payment records.
  • Gift Aid is estimated as 25% of active donations marked eligible.
  • Use the volunteer selector on each section. Trips can also be filtered by date, tax year, vehicle, and status; donations by date, Gift Aid eligibility, status, and method; and payments by date, related donations, and method.
  • Export CSV downloads only the rows currently visible in that table.
  • The financial total cards remain club-wide; the volunteer summary and detail tables can be filtered independently.
  • The volunteer difference is expenses paid minus donations and is informational only; it does not change either record.
